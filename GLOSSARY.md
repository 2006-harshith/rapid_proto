# Rapid Proto Tech website

Marketing site for Rapid Proto Tech India Pvt Ltd, an in-house 3D printing and rapid prototyping company.

## Language

**Service**:
A manufacturing technology RPT offers to customers (SLA, SLS, MJF, SRM), each with its own page.
_Avoid_: Technology (the home page's customer-facing word for the same thing), process

**Service code**:
The short uppercase abbreviation that identifies a Service (e.g. SLA); its page lives at `/services/<code in lowercase>`.
_Avoid_: label, key
